## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Autodesk's office at MaRS, Toronto. A generative model places programs and people from a few inputs; a multi-objective
genetic algorithm searches the space; six objectives score building performance and worker preferences (from a survey
of 250+ employees: distance to colleagues and amenities, daylight, distraction, views). Windows, stairs and elevators
are fixed.

For the thesis: the closest practice precedent to GFit (generative space planning). *(inference)* It reports the
layouts, not how the designers worked with them or changed their way of working.
