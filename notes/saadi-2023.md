## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Six interviews with designers who use commercial generative design tools; a provisional process diagram for early
design. Defining the inputs becomes constraint-driven work: the designer abstracts the problem, then iterates the
inputs on quantitative and qualitative metrics, and that iteration deepens their grasp of problem and solution space.

Same lab, follow-up: the tools take mainly quantitative inputs, so qualitative factors (aesthetics) enter only
indirectly; 34 participants, two tasks, with and without the tools.

For the thesis: the hand moves to the inputs. Product design, not architecture.
