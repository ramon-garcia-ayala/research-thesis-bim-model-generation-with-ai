## 2026-10-18 · Summary
A ladder of what "production" asks of a model, and how much of it a rule can check:

- Geometry + data (walls, grids, areas, uses): yes, GFit already does it.
- Checkable code (egress routes and distances, fire compartments, parking): yes, it is rule checking.
- Coordination (clash detection in Navisworks): only once MEP is in the model.
- Analysis (structural, energy): no; the tool produces the input (grid, envelope), the engineer analyses.
- Specification and QA (materials, site): no, specialists.

Proposed scope for the thesis: "production" reaches as far as a rule can check. Above that, the model hands
over the information the specialist needs. That limit answers the centre question: what can be carried
into production and what cannot.
