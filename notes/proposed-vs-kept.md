## 2026-10-18 · Summary
Generated layouts alone teach an AI the app's own rules back (circular). The value is the difference between
what the tool proposed and what the person kept: an undone Solve says where the rule falls short.

What such a dataset validates:
1. The hypothesis: which decisions people delegate and which they correct, by step and by typology; if offices
   are corrected more, the prediction holds.
2. Learned judgment: approved and corrected layouts are the "approved / rejected" examples an AI needs.

Limits: tens or hundreds of projects serve evaluation and benchmarks, not training; one firm, one typology
(bias); client projects need consent, anonymizing and Gensler's approval. GFit Phase R.2.
