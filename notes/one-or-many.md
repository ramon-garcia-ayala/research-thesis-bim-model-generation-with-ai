## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Two ways of designing. The architect makes one piece and iterates it ten times (one to ten). Computational design
generates ten thousand and filters down to the best five (many to five).

Both answer the same problem: not knowing what the client will change. One-to-ten answers with slack built into one
option (modular spaces, standard measures, room for future changes). Many-to-five answers by regenerating and
filtering again when the change comes. *(inference, to test)* So the contrast is not "manual vs computational" but two
strategies for uncertainty; engineering calls them point-based and set-based design (see sobek-1999).

GFit admits both: from option 1 to 10, or from 10,000 to the best 5.
