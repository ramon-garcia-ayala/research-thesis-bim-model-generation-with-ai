## 2026-10-18 · Summary
The traditional process varies with practice, country and people, so do not compare against "the" tradition:
compare one task (industrial test fit → layout checked for egress and fire compartments).

- Baseline: 1–2 real Gensler projects + interviews with 3–5 people in different roles; count hands, passes, time.
- Against: the same task with GFit.
- The variation between practices is a finding, not a blocker. A case-study method, honest about its reach.

## 2026-10-18 · Method
Scope: from site to the **rule-checked layout** (the limit in production-ready), not construction documents.

Interviews first: people tell their own steps in their own words; then they are crossed with GFit's steps.
Whatever does not match is a finding (client meetings, consultants' input; or steps only the app has).
