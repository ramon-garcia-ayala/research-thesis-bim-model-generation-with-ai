## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Interactive evolutionary design space exploration: the designer picks the parents and sets mutation rate and
generation size, steering the search instead of handing it to an automated solver. Combines structural performance
with the designer's preferences.

Same line: Nathan Brown's MIT work (thesis 2016, dissertation) — multi-objective optimization at concept stage, and
stepping through a design space with estimated gradients, more freedom than automated solvers.

For the thesis: a middle way between one-to-ten and many-to-five; a model for how GFit's optimizer could hand control
back to the designer.
