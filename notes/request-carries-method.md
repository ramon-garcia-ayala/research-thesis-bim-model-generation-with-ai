## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Observed in requests to the author for tools:
- The person who asked for GFit gave the procedure already ordered: grid first, then standard measures, the module,
  the street, the exterior, with modular spaces that allow changes later. Prevention, as a traditional designer would.
- Other requests for workflows arrive with the implementation decided: "first an Excel, then a SharePoint" — the
  tools the office has.

The request carries the method, not only the result. People ask with the tools they know; without a notion of how
computational thinking works, the limit of their language is the limit of what they imagine asking for
(Wittgenstein, *Tractatus* 5.6: "the limits of my language mean the limits of my world").

Open: whether both cases are the same phenomenon (design method vs tool literacy).
