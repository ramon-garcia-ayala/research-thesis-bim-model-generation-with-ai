## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Reflection-in-action: the designer makes a move, watches how the situation answers through its consequences, and
reframes the problem — a "reflective conversation with the situation". Schön's main case is an architecture studio
(an instructor and a student planning an elementary school).

With generative AI: a 2026 paper proposes "reciprocal reflection-in-action", for materials that answer with their own
interpretation; designers judge not only the ideas but the assumptions in how the tool frames the problem. Not about
architects. Authors not checked. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.00280

For the thesis: one-to-ten is a conversation with one drawing; with an optimizer, what answers is a set.
