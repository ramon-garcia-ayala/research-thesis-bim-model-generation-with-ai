## 2026-10-23 · Summary
Designers shown a picture of an existing solution repeat its key features, even when told to avoid them. Fixation:
"a blind adherence to a set of ideas or concepts limiting the output of conceptual design." Tasks: a car bike rack, a
measuring cup for the blind, a spill-proof coffee cup; engineering students and professional engineers, not architects.

Follow-up (Howard et al.): teaching teams about fixation reduced the fixated elements against a control group.
https://research.cbs.dk/en/publications/overcoming-design-fixation-through-education-and-creativity-metho/

With AI (Harvard Design Magazine, "Fixation by Design", Humbi Song; commentary, not a study): the anchor becomes
whatever the tool generates first, with more authority than a sketch, and often with no moment where the designer
notices they went along with it.
https://www.harvarddesignmagazine.org/articles/fixation-by-design-humbi-song-artificial-intelligence/

For the thesis: grid → module → street can be the designer's own anchor; the optimizer can break it or replace it.
Read from summaries, not the paper.
