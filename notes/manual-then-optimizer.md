## 2026-10-23 · Summary
The study: GFit as the instrument of the mindset change, not only the product.
1. Observe how designers use the manual tools they already have (KPI review, site fit, everything done by hand).
2. Once they know the manual tools, offer the optimizer as the next step.
3. Watch whether, and how, they move from one-to-ten to many-to-five: when they open the optimizer, whether they
   use its results, what they keep, what they undo.

What to record: the decision-log build and the proposed-vs-kept data. How to code it: the three loops of bunt-2023
(designer alone, optimizer alone, designer using the optimizer's feedback). Scope: one task (method-one-task).

Who: designers used to one-to-ten — their input says how the tool is used by someone who did not ask for many-to-five.

Earlier thread it absorbs: "how many hands will AI take out of design" is also how people interact with the tool; the
designer is part of the process and adapts to the hands taken out of it. The rooted habits of architectural design and
of construction are what the tool might change.
