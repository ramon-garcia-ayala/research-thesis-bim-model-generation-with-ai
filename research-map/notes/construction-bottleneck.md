## 2026-10-18 · Summary
If firms produce more with fewer resources, they concentrate the market: work is lost between firms, not
within roles. And if design gets faster, the bottleneck moves: lots of designed material, little capacity
to build it (labour, material, permits).

Leads: Theory of Constraints (Goldratt): speed up one step and the bottleneck moves to the next.
Jevons paradox: when something gets more efficient, demand tends to rise instead of work falling.
