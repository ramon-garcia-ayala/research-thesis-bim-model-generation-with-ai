## 2026-11-08 · Summary
Analyzes school buildings from their BIM models to develop new typologies. It describes a software workflow that
translates an IFC file into a graph format (AGraphML), using Revit 2020 and Rhino.Inside, for topological queries.
Generating variations from those graphs, and using the results in Revit, is stated as future work.

For the thesis
- The main risk of being first: it announces nearly the same path. Only partial follow-ups were found (Eisenstadt 2022,
  Langenhan 2023), so the TUM group is worth contacting before claiming the gap.
- It is one typology with a clear reason to study it, a precedent for biasing the method by typology.
- It describes the workflow and the queries. It reports no generated models.
