## 2026-10-22 · Summary
Kyungpook National University. Crawl raster plans from 'Houseplans' (10,000 images from 6,900 projects), define 21
classes (16 spaces, 5 doors/windows/openings) and 3 adjacency cases, annotate bounding boxes by hand, train YOLOv3,
then turn detections into bubble diagrams and vector rows (space, area ratio, door/window flags, weighted adjacency
matrix). Tool AIBIM_Bubblemaker, dataset AIBIM_House.

Results: detection rate 99%, mAP 85%, MIoU 74.2%; laundry, utility and pantry score lowest. US houses from one site
only; boxes cannot describe non-rectangular rooms; areas estimated from ratios; the program is "available upon
request". Names LLMs as a future use of the dataset.

For the thesis
- The one direct challenge: "A unifying aspect of prior studies is a rule-based randomly generated floor plan.
  However, these studies did not consider design theory or knowledge."
- The objection the thesis has to answer. The answer can be that the rule set is the encoded design knowledge
  (codes, typology rules, circulation checks), not random generation (inference).
