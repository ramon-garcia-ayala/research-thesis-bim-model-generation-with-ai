## 2026-11-15 · Summary
Tian, Dong, Zhang, Zhang and Chawla (AAAI 2023). HGMAE is a masked autoencoder for heterogeneous graphs: graphs with
several types of nodes and of edges, each type with its own attributes. GraphMAE masks the features of nodes and ignores
that. HGMAE adds two maskings: metapath masking (the edges of each metapath, a path through typed relations, are masked
and the metapath adjacency matrix is rebuilt) and adaptive attribute masking of the nodes of one target type. In the
attribute masking the mask rate rises linearly during training from 0.5 to 0.8 (a dynamic mask rate), and a share of the
masked nodes is left unchanged or replaced by random tokens. A third task predicts a positional feature of each node. The
encoder and decoder are HAN. It is tested on four academic and knowledge graphs (DBLP, Freebase, ACM, AMiner) for node
classification and node clustering, against 10 baselines, with mean and standard deviation over 10 seeds.

Results
- Dynamic mask rate against fixed rates (Fig. 3): it gives the best result on DBLP and on Freebase. A fixed rate does not
  carry over between graphs: 0.9 is the best fixed rate for Freebase and performs poorly on DBLP.
- Ablation, Micro-F1 (Table 3), full model against the one without metapath-based edge reconstruction: DBLP 92.73 against
  91.99, Freebase 66.61 against 66.01, ACM 90.59 against 76.85, AMiner 81.45 against 72.12. Removing the edge task hurts
  most; removing the attribute task or the positional task also lowers every row.
- Node clustering (Table 2): against the best baseline, HeCo, NMI improves by 17% and ARI by 25% on ACM, and by 27% and
  33% on AMiner. In classification (Table 1) it is best in most cases and second best on AMiner against HeCo on a few
  F1 values.
- Leaving unchanged and replacing always helped (Fig. 4), the opposite of what GraphMAE states. Best rates: 0.3 and 0.1 on
  DBLP, 0.0 and 0.1 on Freebase.
- Hidden size: 256 or 512 is enough; 1024 lowers the result (Fig. 5).

For the thesis
- The closest masking recipe to a BIM graph with kinds of element and kinds of relation (host, contained in, adjacent):
  typed nodes, typed edges and per-type attributes are each masked in their own way (masking-training), and the typed
  graph is the shape the deconstruction in deconstruct-for-training also assumes.
- The lesson on the rate is the one to carry: the best rate depends on the graph, and a schedule that rises from 0.5 to
  0.8 avoids choosing one. The thesis can sweep the rate or use a schedule instead of fixing a number from another domain.
- The graphs are citation and knowledge graphs, not buildings, and the tasks are node classification and clustering of the
  embeddings; nothing is generated and nothing is checked for validity. How well the masking carries from academic
  graphs to element graphs is not tested here.
- The metapaths are an input of the graph definition (the set Φ), given before training; in a Revit graph they would be
  sequences such as wall, host, door, which the thesis would have to define (an inference, not in the paper).
- Code is released at https://github.com/meettyj/HGMAE.
