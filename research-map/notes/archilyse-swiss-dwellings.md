## 2026-11-15 · Summary
Source of datasets: [Archilyse Research, Swiss Dwellings Dataset & Data Pipeline](https://archilyse.standfest.science/).
The page is the one the author gave; only its title was read (the content loads with JavaScript, so a script saw no
more). It is the place to look for the Swiss Dwellings floor plans and for the pipeline that prepares them.

For the thesis
- MSD, already in the library, is the paper of a multi-apartment floor-plan benchmark; one of its authors is Standfest,
  and the page is on a standfest.science address, so it probably belongs to the same work (an inference from the name,
  not read on the page). MaskLayout (zhang-2026) trains on 43,000 apartments parsed from SwissDwellings, so this is also
  where its data come from.
- It is a source of plans, not of Revit or IFC models: it does not give the element graphs of masking-training. Use it
  as the example of what a public dataset and its preparation pipeline look like next to real-or-synthetic-data and
  dataset-purposes.
