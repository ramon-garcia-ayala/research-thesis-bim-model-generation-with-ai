## 2026-11-08 · Summary
A graph-based framework that finds candidate prefabricated modules across several projects by frequent pattern mining
(gSpan), implemented as add-ons in a BIM environment. The test data are ten floor plans the authors made from two
standard module types of a collaborating company, residential timber, altered on purpose to test robustness.

Results: the repeated patterns share a degree of standardization and can be considered module candidates.

For the thesis
- The only mining of reusable parts across BIM projects found: a precedent for "decompose into meaningful parts".
- The modules were planted in the data by construction, so finding them again is a weak test on real models.
- It stops at the library of modules: nothing new is generated.
